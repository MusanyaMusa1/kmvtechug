Reader Contributions

Send Us a Story

You are often the first to know what is happening in your community. If you have witnessed something that matters, tell us. Our editors read every submission, and the strongest stories are published with the credit you choose.

Before you send

  • Tell us what happened, where, and when. Stick to facts we can check.
  • Give us a phone number or WhatsApp. An editor will contact you to verify the story before anything is published. Your contact details are only used by our editors and are never published.
  • Only send photos and videos you took yourself, or have permission to share.
  • We may edit stories for length, clarity and accuracy, and we may decline stories we cannot verify.

What happens next

  1. An editor reads your story.
  2. We contact you to confirm the details.
  3. If it is published, it is marked as a reader contribution, credited the way you asked: your full name, your first name and district, or anonymous.
