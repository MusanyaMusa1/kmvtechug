About King Musah Media

Ugandan news, told in English and Luganda.

King Musah Media is a Ugandan news outlet, founded in 2020. We report news, politics, entertainment, sports and business from Uganda, and bring you the world's biggest stories in our International section.

What we cover

News, Politics, Entertainment, Sports, Business, International. Many of our stories are in both English and Luganda.

How we work

An editor reviews every story before it is published. We name our sources, we correct our mistakes in public, and we are open about how we use technology, including AI. Please read our editorial standards and our corrections policy.

How we are funded

We are funded by advertising, and we plan to add paid newsletter subscriptions and sponsorship from businesses. Sponsored content will always be clearly labelled, and no advertiser or sponsor decides what we report.
