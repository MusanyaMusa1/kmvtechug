FORMER MP MUWANGA KIVUMBI ARRAIGNED IN COURT AFTER ABDUCTION CLAIMS

KM
King Musah Media News Desk
Published 29 July 2026 · English
📷 Muwanga Kivumbi in court

Former Butambala County Member of Parliament Muhammad Muwanga Kivumbi has been charged with incitement to violence and remanded to prison after appearing before the Butambala Magistrates Court on Wednesday. The development follows his dramatic reappearance after over two weeks of being missing.ABDUCTION ON CAMERA AND OFFICIAL DENIALSKivumbi was reportedly intercepted in Mpigi on 9 July by police before armed men in military attire took him away. Video footage of the incident circulated widely on social media. Both the Uganda Police and the Uganda Peoples Defence Forces subsequently denied knowledge of his whereabouts or involvement in any abduction. The UPDF in particular filed an affidavit stating that checks in all gazetted detention facilities found no trace of the former legislator.His family and political allies raised alarm over the disappearance describing it as an enforced disappearance. A habeas corpus application was filed in the High Court prompting judicial directives for security agencies to produce him or explain his status.REAPPEARANCE AND COURT PROCEEDINGSKivumbi resurfaced when he was dropped off at Nateete Police Station by unknown operatives. He was then transferred and arraigned in Butambala where the court remanded him. The case has been adjourned to 11 August for further mention. Opposition leaders including those from the National Unity Platform have condemned the events as part of a pattern of political persecution and questioned the credibility of state institutions. They demand accountability for the period he was held incommunicado.BALANCED PERSPECTIVE ON RULE OF LAWSecurity agencies have not issued detailed public statements on the specific allegations of abduction. Judicial processes continue with the High Court scheduled to deliver a ruling on related applications soon. Legal experts emphasise the importance of due process transparency and protection of individual rights in such high profile matters regardless of political affiliation.The case continues to fuel public debate on issues of personal liberty state accountability and the administration of justice in Uganda.

King Musah Media will follow subsequent court appearances and provide factual updates.
