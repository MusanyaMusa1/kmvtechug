Parliament Vets Juma Witonze Kisekka as New Minister of State for Internal Affairs

KM
King Musah Media News Desk
Published 30 July 2026 · English
📷 Paliament Vetting

Kampala, Uganda

Uganda’s Parliament Appointments Committee has successfully vetted Hon. Juma Witonze Kisekka, President Yoweri Museveni’s nominee for Minister of State for Internal Affairs. The process, completed on Thursday morning, paves the way for his formal appointment following a swift review of his credentials.

Chaired by Speaker Jacob Oboth Oboth, the committee included Deputy Speaker Thomas Tayebwa and other lawmakers. They examined Kisekka’s documents and suitability for the sensitive internal security docket. With no major issues raised, the panel will now submit its report to the President, who nominated him three days ago. Kisekka is expected to be sworn in soon after presidential assent.

Background and Political Context

Kisekka, a dedicated National Resistance Movement (NRM) figure and Chief Coordinator of the Patriotic League of Uganda (PLU) for the Greater Mpigi region, hails from Gomba. His nomination comes after Parliament rejected an earlier candidate, Dr Lawrence Muganga, primarily over unresolved questions regarding multiple citizenships and documentation.The Internal Affairs docket is critical in Uganda, overseeing police, immigration, prisons, and overall internal security. Observers view Kisekka’s selection as a strategic move to install an experienced loyalist with diplomatic and grassroots mobilisation credentials.During the session, committee members probed Kisekka on his vision for the ministry, governance experience, and plans to address key challenges such as public safety and border management. Sources close to the process described the vetting as thorough yet cordial, reflecting the committee’s mandate under parliamentary rules.Kisekka expressed gratitude for the opportunity, reaffirming his commitment to national service. His approval is widely expected to sail through given the ruling party’s dominance in the Appointments Committee.

Implications for Cabinet and Governance

This development continues Museveni’s pattern of timely cabinet adjustments to maintain stability within the executive. It also highlights Parliament’s oversight role, as demonstrated by the earlier rejection of Muganga — a rare instance of the legislature pushing back on a presidential nomination.For the Greater Mpigi area and broader central region, Kisekka’s elevation strengthens local representation at the national level. Critics, however, may question the pace and depth of such appointments in addressing long-standing security and governance concerns.King Musah Media will monitor Kisekka’s swearing-in and early priorities in the Internal Affairs ministry.
