📷 CCTV Footage Shows People Taking Cover During Riyadh Airport Missile Strike

According to reporting by BBC News, closed-circuit television footage from inside Riyadh airport has captured the moment a missile struck the facility on Saturday.

The video shows individuals inside the terminal building quickly taking cover as the strike occurred, reflecting the sudden panic at the airport during the event.

Specific details regarding casualties, the source of the projectile, or the full extent of structural damage were not immediately detailed in the video release.

Readers can find the full original report and view the video footage directly at BBC News.
