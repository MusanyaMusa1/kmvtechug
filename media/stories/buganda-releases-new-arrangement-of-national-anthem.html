BUGANDA RELEASES NEW ARRANGEMENT OF NATIONAL ANTHEM

KM
King Musah Media News Desk
Published 29 July 2026 · English
📷 Katikiro Charles Peter Mayiga

The Katikkiro of Buganda Charles Peter Mayiga has officially released a refreshed version of the kingdom's national anthem Ekitiibwa kya Buganda. The announcement came through a post on X today featuring a performance by the St. Charles Lwanga Lubaga Cathedral Choir.TRADITION MEETS PROFESSIONAL ARRANGEMENTComposed in 1939 by Rev. Polycarp Kakooza the anthem known in English as The Pride of Buganda holds deep significance for the people of Buganda. The new edition was arranged by music experts and rendered entirely in the Luganda language. It showcases the full vocal range of the choir with clear soprano alto tenor and bass sections supported by keyboards saxophone and traditional drums.Mayiga described the project as an effort to preserve and promote Buganda's cultural heritage through high quality production. The video performance recorded outdoors with the choir in coordinated attire highlights both musical excellence and cultural pride.POSITIVE RECEPTION FROM THE PUBLICEarly reactions on social media have been largely favorable. Users praised the improved pronunciations harmonious delivery and overall production quality. Several commenters requested that the anthem be made available on digital platforms such as Spotify and iTunes to allow wider access and international reach.This release comes at a time when cultural institutions across Uganda continue to explore ways to keep traditional expressions relevant for younger generations while maintaining their authenticity.LOOKING AHEAD FOR BUGANDAKingdom officials have not yet detailed further plans for distribution or additional cultural projects. However the initiative underscores ongoing efforts to strengthen Buganda's identity within the broader national context.King Musah Media will continue to monitor developments and provide updates on this and other stories of cultural importance.
