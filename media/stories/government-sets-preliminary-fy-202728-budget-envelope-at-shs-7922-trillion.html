Government Sets Preliminary FY 2027/28 Budget Envelope at Shs 79.22 Trillion

KM
King Musah Media News Desk
Published 18 September 2026 · English
📷 Permanent Secretary Ramathan Ggoobi announcing the FY 2027/28 budget resource envelope

The Ministry of Finance, Planning and Economic Development has set a preliminary resource envelope of Shs 79.22 trillion for the 2027/28 Financial Year, marking a reduction of Shs 5.17 trillion from the current financial year's Shs 84.39 trillion allocation.

The figures were officially communicated by the Permanent Secretary and Secretary to the Treasury, Ramathan Ggoobi, through the First Budget Call Circular dispatched to Accounting Officers, Chief Executive Officers of State-Owned Enterprises, and Public Corporations.

Shift towards fiscal discipline. Mr. Ggoobi explained that the reduced spending baseline reflects a deliberate strategy toward sustainable self-reliance. The tighter ceiling is designed to force stronger domestic revenue mobilization while curbing reliance on external financing and high-interest borrowing to keep national debt sustainable.

Planning for the upcoming financial year will be driven by seven strategic shifts detailed in the circular. These priorities include revenue-led fiscal consolidation, prudent management of oil revenues, private capital mobilization, and accelerated implementation of the national Tenfold Growth Strategy. Accounting officers across local governments and central agencies have been directed to align their Budget Framework Papers accordingly.

Balanced perspective. While economic analysts and civil society organizations have welcomed the commitment to curb public debt and enforce fiscal discipline, concerns remain over the potential impact on public service delivery. Critics argue that a reduced budget baseline could strain funding for critical sectors such as healthcare, education, and district road infrastructure if domestic revenue collection targets are not fully realized.

As government agencies prepare their respective budget framework submissions in line with the official process calendar, King Musah Media will continue to monitor developments and track the budget implementation process.
