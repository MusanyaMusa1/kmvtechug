📷 Pope Leo to Undergo Surgery Following Lung Nodule Discovery

According to reporting by BBC News, Pope Leo is scheduled to undergo surgery following the discovery of a lump on his lung.

The 71-year-old pontiff stated that the nodule was identified "at a very early stage," allowing medical professionals to address the condition promptly.

Further details regarding the surgical procedure and recovery plan remain under official medical management as the pontiff prepares for treatment.

Readers can find the full original report on BBC News.
