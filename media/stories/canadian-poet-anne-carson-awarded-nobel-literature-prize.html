📷 Canadian Poet Anne Carson Awarded Nobel Literature Prize

Canadian poet Anne Carson has been awarded the Nobel Prize in Literature, according to reporting by BBC News. Described as 'bold and inventive,' the 76-year-old writer has earned international recognition for her distinct contributions to modern literature.

Carson is widely celebrated for works that frequently cross traditional boundaries between poetry, fiction, and scholarship, blending multiple disciplines within her writing.

Readers can find the full original report on BBC News.
