Ogwang Assures Parliament that Aki-Bua Stadium and Other Venues Will Meet CAF Standards

KM
King Musah Media News Desk
Published 05 August 2026 · English
📷 Sports Minister Peter Ogwang speaking in Parliament

Sports Minister Peter Ogwang has assured Parliament that government is on course to deliver three stadiums to host the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON). Speaking during plenary, Ogwang said he wrote to the Confederation of African Football (CAF) requesting an inspection of Aki-Bua Stadium. CAF agreed that the facility can host AFCON matches if government commits to delivering the required standards. “The contractor is working at 120 percent to beat the December deadline,” the Minister told MPs. Uganda is co-hosting AFCON 2027 with other East African nations. The successful delivery of modern, CAF-compliant stadiums remains a key requirement for the tournament. Ogwang’s statement comes amid ongoing public interest in the readiness of sports infrastructure ahead of the continental showpiece.
