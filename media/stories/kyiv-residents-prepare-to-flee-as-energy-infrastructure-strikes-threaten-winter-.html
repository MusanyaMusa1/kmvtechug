📷 Kyiv Residents Prepare to Flee as Energy Infrastructure Strikes Threaten Winter Power

According to reporting by BBC News, a renewed campaign of Russian air strikes targeting power and energy infrastructure has forced many residents in Ukraine's capital, Kyiv, to contemplate leaving the city.

The renewed bombardment of critical energy facilities comes at a crucial time as winter approaches, raising significant concerns about access to reliable power and heating during the impending cold season.

With essential services facing heightened disruption, an increasing number of individuals and families in the capital are preparing to pack their belongings and relocate to areas with better infrastructure support.

Readers can find the full original report on this story at BBC News.
