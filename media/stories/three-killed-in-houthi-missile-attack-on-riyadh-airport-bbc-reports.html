📷 Three Killed in Houthi Missile Attack on Riyadh Airport, BBC Reports

According to reporting by BBC News, three Saudi nationals have been killed following missile attacks targeting the airport in Riyadh, carried out by the Houthi movement.

The strike on the capital's airport comes amid a recent escalation in fighting between the Iran-backed Houthi group and Saudi Arabia, which leads a coalition supporting Yemen's government.

The incident underscores the ongoing cross-border security challenges and heightened geopolitical tensions currently affecting the region.

The full original report on this story can be accessed on the BBC News website.
