📷 Satellite Images Reveal Luxury Boat Near Kim Jong Un's Summer Retreat

According to a recent report by BBC News, newly analyzed satellite imagery has provided a rare glimpse into the leisure activities of North Korean leader Kim Jong Un. The images highlight a lavish private vessel docked near one of his primary summer compounds.

The high-resolution photos show that the luxury boat is outfitted with high-end recreational features, including a 50-meter swimming pool and multiple water slides. These leisure amenities offer a stark visual contrast to the economic realities and daily living conditions experienced by the general North Korean population.

International analysts frequently monitor satellite data over key North Korean sites to gain insight into the leadership's movements and lifestyle patterns. The presence and positioning of the vessel indicate its active use during the holiday season at the coastal estate.

Readers can find the full original report and view the satellite images on the BBC News website.

Source: BBC News
