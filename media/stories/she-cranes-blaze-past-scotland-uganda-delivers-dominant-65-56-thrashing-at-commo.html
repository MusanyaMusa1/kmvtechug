She Cranes Blaze Past Scotland! Uganda Delivers Dominant 65-56 Thrashing at Commonwealth Games

KM
King Musah Media News Desk
Published 30 July 2026 · English
📷 She Cranes performance in common wealth games

Glasgow, Scotland — Uganda’s She Cranes delivered a commanding performance in netball as they defeated hosts Scotland 65-56 in a Pool B encounter at the Commonwealth Games. The win showcased the growing strength and tactical maturity of Ugandan women’s netball on the global stage.

Playing with pace, precision, and defensive intensity, the She Cranes controlled large portions of the match against a resilient but fatigued Scottish side. Uganda’s attacking unit combined slick movement and accurate shooting, while their defence disrupted Scotland’s flow throughout the contest at the Hydro arena.This result adds to Uganda’s impressive run at the Games, following strong victories over Wales and Trinidad & Tobago. The She Cranes’ attacking flair and defensive organisation have been standout features, with the team posting a record 80-goal haul in an earlier match.Key Highlights from the Match Uganda maintained pressure from the opening whistle, building leads through coordinated play.

Scotland fought back in patches, showing home determination, but struggled to contain Uganda’s attacking threats.

The She Cranes’ bench contributed effectively, ensuring momentum never wavered significantly.

Uganda’s Rising Star in Netball

The She Cranes’ success reflects years of investment in women’s sport in Uganda. Netball has gained significant popularity, with domestic leagues and grassroots programmes producing talented athletes capable of competing at the highest level.This victory not only boosts Uganda’s medal prospects but also inspires young girls across the country to take up the sport. President Museveni and sports officials have consistently highlighted the importance of women’s achievements in elevating national pride.Head coach and technical team will now focus on recovery and preparation for remaining fixtures, aiming to progress deep into the knockout stages and challenge traditional powerhouses like Australia, New Zealand, and Jamaica.Proud

Moment for Ugandan Sports

Uganda’s women continue to shine at these Games — from netball triumphs to historic medals in other disciplines. The She Cranes’ performance against Scotland epitomises resilience, skill, and national spirit.Congratulations to the entire squad and technical bench. Uganda is making its mark in Glasgow!
