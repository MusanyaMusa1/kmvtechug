📷 Rare Pink French Royal Diamond Goes on Display Century After Heist

According to reporting by BBC News, a historic French royal jewel featuring a rare pink diamond has gone on public display in France for the first time in nearly a century.

The precious gem was stolen in 1926 during an amateur heist. Following the theft, the royal jewel was remarkably recovered after investigators found it hidden inside an apple.

The new public exhibition marks the first time the historic artifact has been accessible to visitors since its recovery, celebrating its return nearly 100 years after the dramatic disappearance.

Readers can find the full original report on BBC News.
