Deputy Speaker Commends Career of Integrity at Launch of Odongo’s Autobiography

KM
King Musah Media News Desk
Published 15 August 2026 · English
📷 Deputy Speaker Thomas Tayebwa speaking at Dan Odongo’s book launch

Deputy Speaker of Parliament Thomas Tayebwa has praised the career of Uganda National Examinations Board (UNEB) Executive Director Dan Odongo, describing it as exemplary and worthy of emulation by public servants across the country. Tayebwa made the remarks on Friday evening while speaking at the launch of Odongo’s book, titled From Humble Beginnings: The Journey of a Teacher Who Rose to Lead a Nation’s Trust, held at Hotel Africana in Kampala. He said Odongo’s record at UNEB demonstrated what public service should truly be about. “Leadership is not about titles; it is about service, honesty, and commitment to the people one leads. A title may open the door, but only one’s actions earn lasting respect,” the Deputy Speaker stated. Odongo has led UNEB through several examination cycles and has been widely associated with efforts to maintain the integrity of national assessments. His journey from a classroom teacher to the helm of one of the country’s most sensitive public institutions forms the core of the newly launched autobiography. The book launch brought together education stakeholders, public officials and well-wishers who turned up to celebrate Odongo’s contribution to the education sector. In a public service environment often criticised for inefficiency and lack of accountability, recognition of long-serving officers who maintain professional standards carries particular weight. Tayebwa’s endorsement places emphasis on character and results over mere position. King Musah Media congratulates Dan Odongo on the launch of his book and on a career that has drawn national recognition.
