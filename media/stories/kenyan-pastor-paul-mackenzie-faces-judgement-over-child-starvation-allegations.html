📷 Kenyan Pastor Paul Mackenzie Faces Judgement Over Child Starvation Allegations

A judicial verdict is expected today in Kenya for pastor Paul Mackenzie, who stands accused of ordering parents to starve their children to death, according to reporting by BBC Africa.

State prosecutors allege that Mackenzie managed to continue directing his followers even while incarcerated. Investigators assert that he lured the latest victims to their deaths by writing notes directly from his prison cell.

The high-profile legal proceedings mark a crucial moment in addressing the tragic events linked to the controversial pastor's actions in Kenya.

Readers can find the full original report at BBC Africa.

Source: BBC Africa
