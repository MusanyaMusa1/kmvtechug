📷 Anti-Putin Covert Operations Inside Russia Highlighted in New Report

According to a report published by BBC News, individuals operating within Russia are carrying out covert sabotage operations aimed at supporting Ukrainian intelligence during the ongoing war.

In the report, two men shared details regarding their alleged involvement in clandestine activities intended to disrupt Russian military efforts and oppose President Vladimir Putin from inside the country.

Their accounts offer insight into the hidden opposition networks functioning within Russian borders to assist Ukraine during the conflict.

Readers can find the full original report on the BBC News website.
