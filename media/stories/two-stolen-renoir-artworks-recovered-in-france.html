📷 Two Stolen Renoir Artworks Recovered in France

According to reporting by BBC News, two paintings by Pierre-Auguste Renoir that were stolen from a museum last month have now been recovered.

The artworks were taken during a break-in at the Renoir Museum, located on the French Riviera. The perpetrators carried out the theft by cutting through a perimeter fence and smashing a window to gain entry to the facility.

While further specifics regarding the recovery operation have not been detailed, the retrieval marks a resolution to the theft of the two valuable pieces.

Readers can find the full original report at BBC News.
