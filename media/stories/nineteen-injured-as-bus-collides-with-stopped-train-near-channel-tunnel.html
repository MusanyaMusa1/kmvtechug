📷 Nineteen Injured as Bus Collides with Stopped Train Near Channel Tunnel

Nineteen people have been injured after a bus collided with a stationary train on the French side of the Channel Tunnel, according to reporting by BBC News.

Following the incident, emergency services responded to the scene, and officials confirmed that two people were airlifted to the hospital to receive treatment.

Authorities reported that despite the number of injuries sustained in the collision, no one's life appeared to be in danger.

Readers can find the full original report on this incident at BBC News.

Source: BBC News
