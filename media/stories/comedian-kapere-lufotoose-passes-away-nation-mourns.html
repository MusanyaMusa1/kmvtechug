Comedian Kapere Lufotoose Passes Away, Nation Mourns

KM
King Musah Media News Desk
Published 01 November 2026 · English
📷 File photo of late comedian Kapere Lufotoose

Ugandan comedian Yokana Mbuuse, widely known as Kapere Lufotoose (also referred to as Kapere Lufotosi), has passed away.

News of his sudden death broke on Sunday and quickly spread across social media, with radio stations and political figures confirming the loss. Galaxy FM was among the first media houses to announce: “JUST IN: Comedian Kapere has passed away! May His Soul Rest In Peace.”

NUP Updates described him as a comrade, while Manjiya County MP and NUP Acting President John Baptist Nambeshe issued a detailed tribute.

“It is with profound sadness that we mourn the passing of Yokana Mbuuse, popularly known as Kapere Lufotoose, a gifted comedian whose talent brought smiles, laughter and joy to countless Ugandans,” Nambeshe said.

“Through his comedy, he did more than entertain. He used humour as a powerful tool to communicate messages of change, hope and social awareness. He reminded us that even in difficult times, laughter can give people strength and a reason to keep believing in a better tomorrow.”

Nambeshe noted that beyond the stage, Kapere carried political aspirations and had planned to contest as a councillor in Makindye Municipality Division under the National Unity Platform. He described him as “a talented entertainer, a dreamer and a son of the soil who touched many lives in his own unique way.”

Many Ugandans have taken to social media to celebrate Kapere’s sharp wit and ability to make people laugh while highlighting everyday struggles. He leaves behind a legacy of comedy that resonated with ordinary citizens.

May his soul rest in eternal peace.
