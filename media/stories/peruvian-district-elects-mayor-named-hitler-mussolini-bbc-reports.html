📷 Peruvian District Elects Mayor Named Hitler Mussolini, BBC Reports

In reporting published by BBC News, a left-wing candidate named Hitler Mussolini has won a mayoral election in Peru.

Exit polls indicate that the candidate secured a narrow victory in a remote district situated in the Peruvian Andes.

The election results follow a tightly contested vote for the local leadership position in the region.

The full original report can be found on the BBC News website.
