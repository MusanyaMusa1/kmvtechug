NSSF Assets Grow to Shs32.8 Trillion as Long-Term Investment Push Continues

KM
King Musah Media News Desk
Published 15 July 2026 · English
📷 Financial charts and documents on a desk

The National Social Security Fund has reported assets rising to Shs32.8 trillion, signalling strong performance as government encourages long-term investment capital to support national development goals.

Officials highlighted the fund's role in providing stable financing for infrastructure and other priority sectors under the new budget, as Uganda advances its economic targets for the coming years.

The growth is expected to boost confidence among savers and investors amid the government's ongoing monetisation efforts.
