PM Nabbanja Joins Buganda Kingdom for Historic #Amatikkira33 Celebrations at Watoto Church

KM
King Musah Media News Desk
Published 31 July 2026 · English
📷 Prime Minister reaching at watoto church for 33rd coronation

Bweyogerere, Uganda

— Prime Minister Robinah Nabbanja on Friday led government representation at Watoto Church in Bweyogerere as the Buganda Kingdom marked the 33rd anniversary of the coronation of Kabaka Ronald Muwenda Mutebi II.

The colourful #Amatikkira33 celebrations brought together cultural leaders, government officials, clergy, and thousands of Baganda subjects to honour the Kabaka’s enduring reign and contributions to peace, development, and cultural preservation in Uganda.

Dressed in traditional attire, PM Nabbanja was warmly received as she arrived for the commemorative service. The event highlighted the strong synergy between the central government and traditional institutions in fostering national unity and community development.

Significance of Amatikkira33

Kabaka Mutebi II ascended the throne on July 31, 1993, restoring the Buganda Kingdom’s cultural monarchy after years of abolition. Over the past 33 years, he has spearheaded numerous initiatives in education, health, agriculture, and youth empowerment through the kingdom’s structures, significantly impacting millions of subjects across Buganda and beyond.The anniversary celebrations serve as a moment of reflection on the Kabaka’s visionary leadership, which emphasises moral values, land stewardship, and harmonious coexistence. Watoto Church, known for its community outreach, provided a fitting venue for this year’s commemorative prayers and thanksgiving.

Excerpt:

“A strong cultural institution working hand in hand with government brings development closer to the people.” — Remarks from government sources during the Amatikkira33 event.Government-Kingdom PartnershipThe presence of the Prime Minister underscores the Museveni administration’s commitment to respecting and collaborating with Uganda’s traditional leaders. Buganda remains one of the most influential kingdoms, playing a key role in social mobilisation and service delivery.As the celebrations continue across Buganda, focus will turn to the Kabaka’s ongoing projects and the kingdom’s vision for the next phase of its restoration and growth. Subjects and well-wishers have used the occasion to express loyalty and gratitude to Kabaka Mutebi II for his steady guidance.King Musah Media extends congratulations to His Majesty the Kabaka on this milestone anniversary. Long live the King!
