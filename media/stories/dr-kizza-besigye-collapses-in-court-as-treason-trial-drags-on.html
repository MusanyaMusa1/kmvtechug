DR KIZZA BESIGYE COLLAPSES IN COURT AS TREASON TRIAL DRAGS ON

KM
King Musah Media News Desk
Published 29 July 2026 · English
📷 Kizza Byesigye collapses in court

Retired Colonel Kizza Besigye was rushed to hospital on Wednesday after he fainted in the High Court during his ongoing treason trial. Court proceedings were immediately suspended. The incident has once again thrown the spotlight on the prolonged legal battle and the political tensions surrounding the opposition veteran.HEALTH DETERIORATION IN CUSTODYBesigye who has been in detention since his arrest last year collapsed while objecting to the continuation of the trial without his preferred legal team. Eyewitness accounts and video footage from the courtroom show the accused man protesting the appointment of state lawyers by Justice Emmanuel Baguma before he fell ill. Prison officers had handcuffed him and surrounded him as the session continued.Medical sources indicate that Besigye has faced health challenges while in custody. His supporters argue that the extended detention without bail has contributed to his deteriorating condition.LONG TRIAL AND LEGAL HURDLESThe treason case against Besigye and his co-accused has stretched for months with repeated delays. The opposition leader and his allies maintain that the charges are politically motivated. Critics of the process point to the arrest of his lead counsel and the denial of entry into Uganda for Kenyan lawyer and former Justice Minister Martha Karua who was expected to join the defence team.The judiciary has defended its handling of the matter citing security concerns and procedural requirements including new guidelines on court access that limited observers and journalists.PRESIDENTIAL REMARKS ON DELAYSPresident Yoweri Museveni has publicly stated that Besigye himself is responsible for the slow pace of the trial rather than the courts. The President described the situation as one where the accused employs tactics to prolong proceedings. Opposition voices reject this view and accuse the state of using the justice system to sideline political rivals.BALANCED CALLS FOR RESOLUTIONLegal experts and human rights observers have called for a fair and expeditious hearing that respects due process and the right to legal representation of choice. As Besigye receives medical attention questions remain about his fitness to stand trial and the broader implications for Uganda's political landscape.King Musah Media will continue monitoring the case and provide balanced updates as developments unfold.
