King Saha Packs Kololo to Capacity at Tondeka E’Kololo Festival

KM
King Musah Media News Desk
Published 14 August 2026 · English
📷 Massive crowd at King Saha’s Tondeka E’Kololo Festival

Musician King Saha has delivered one of the biggest turnouts of the year after packing Kololo Airstrip to capacity for his highly anticipated Tondeka E’Kololo Festival on Friday, 14 August 2026. From afternoon into the evening, revellers streamed into the venue in large numbers. Photos and videos circulating on social media show the grounds filled with fans dressed in their best, creating a colourful atmosphere of music, fashion and high energy. New Vision described the scene as Kololo having “surrendered” to King Saha, with the crowd behaving as if it was already midnight long before the main performance. The concert comes just three days after the singer was arrested in a dramatic raid on his Kyanja residence that involved reports of gunfire. He was later released. The successful turnout has been widely interpreted as a strong show of support from his fan base. Supporters waved flags and chanted as the energy built up. Some sections of the crowd were heard raising political slogans, reflecting the charged atmosphere that often accompanies major public gatherings in Uganda. Yet the dominant mood remained celebratory as fans turned up to enjoy the music. King Saha had spent the preceding days aggressively promoting the show, moving around Kampala and selling tickets. Ordinary tickets were priced at UGX 20,000 and VIP at UGX 50,000, with tables going for higher amounts. The heavy attendance suggests the marketing paid off and that the artiste retains significant pulling power in the industry. For Uganda’s entertainment scene, a sold-out or near-capacity show at Kololo remains a major achievement. Few local artistes consistently fill the historic airstrip. King Saha’s ability to draw such numbers so soon after a high-profile security incident underlines both his popularity and the resilience of his brand. As the night progressed, the focus stayed on the performance and the collective energy of thousands of fans who turned up to celebrate.
