Lukwago, a vocal opposition figure and president of the People's Front for Freedom, denies the charges. Health deterioration and Mulago report. Lukwago's remand has been marked by serious health complications. Following his arrest on June 15, 2026, reports emerged of a hypertension crisis (readings reportedly reaching 217/100) and aggravated spinal issues. He was transferred to Mulago National Referral Hospital after a court order, where a panel of senior consultants assessed him. The Mulago medical report confirms multiple chronic conditions: well-controlled hypertension, unresolved post-COVID-19 lung disease with mild bronchiectasis, persistent tinnitus and loss of smell, residual cervical radiculitis post-spinal surgery in India, and non-erosive gastroesophageal reflux disease with hiatus hernia.

Doctors recommended continued medication, a well-ventilated environment, ongoing ear treatment, and potential further evaluation by his Indian spine surgeons. Lukwago has remained frail, in pain, and under treatment, with his legal team describing him as weak while praising Mulago's care within its capacity; plans for possible transfer abroad for specialised care have been discussed.
