President Museveni Appoints Hon. Juma Witonze Kisekka as Minister of State for Internal Affairs

KM
King Musah Media News Desk
Published 27 July 2026 · English
📷 Jumah watenga

In a latest cabinet adjustment, President Yoweri Kaguta Museveni has appointed Hon. Juma Witonze Kisekka (also referred to as Juma Witonze) as the new Minister of State for Internal Affairs. The appointment, announced on July 27, 2026, is pending parliamentary approval, in line with constitutional requirements.

The move comes amid ongoing government efforts to strengthen internal security, immigration management, and overall governance in a key ministry that oversees critical functions including police, prisons, immigration, and disaster preparedness.

Who is Hon. Juma Witonze Kisekka?

Hon. Witonze is a known mobilizer within the National Resistance Movement (NRM), particularly active in the Central Region. He has been associated with party structures and grassroots mobilization efforts. His elevation to a full ministerial role marks a significant step in his political career, transitioning from behind-the-scenes organizing to a high-profile executive position.
