Nation Media Group Uganda Cleared to Resume Operations After June Shutdown

KM
King Musah Media News Desk
Published 28 July 2026 · English
📷 letter showing Ntv is allowed to operate

In a significant development for media freedom in Uganda, Nation Media Group (NMG) Uganda publishers of Daily Monitor and operators of NTV Uganda and other platforms, has been cleared to resume full operations following a government-mandated shutdown in June 2026.

The announcement, which has sparked widespread reactions across social media, marks the end of a tense period that raised concerns about press freedom, regulatory compliance, and the role of independent media in the country.

What We Know So Far

NMG Uganda’s outlets, including the influential Daily Monitor newspaper and NTV television, were taken off air and out of circulation in June.

The clearance to resume comes directly from relevant authorities, allowing the group to restart broadcasting, printing, and digital operations.

As of Tuesday, July 28, 2026, the public is already celebrating the return of a major independent voice, with many expressing relief and high expectations for robust journalism.
