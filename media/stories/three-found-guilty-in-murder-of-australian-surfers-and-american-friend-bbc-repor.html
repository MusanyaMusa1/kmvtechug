📷 Three Found Guilty in Murder of Australian Surfers and American Friend, BBC Reports

Three men have been found guilty of the murders of two Australian surfer brothers and their American friend, according to reporting by BBC News.

The victims were identified as brothers Jake and Callum Robinson, alongside their US friend Carter Rhoad.

Their bodies were discovered by authorities at the bottom of a well following an investigation into the incident.

Readers can find the full original report on this story at BBC News.
