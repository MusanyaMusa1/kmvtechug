📷 Armed Parent and Teacher Intervene in Oregon School Attack, BBC Reports

According to reporting by BBC News, a 16-year-old suspect targeted a special educational needs classroom at an Oregon primary school before being confronted by staff and a parent.

Police report that the teenager previously attended the primary school. During the incident, a teacher engaged in a struggle with the attacker inside the classroom.

An armed father subsequently intervened, shooting the suspect multiple times to halt the attack, according to law enforcement officials.

Readers can find the full original report on BBC News.

Source: BBC News
