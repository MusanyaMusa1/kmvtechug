📷 Emergency Records Reveal Confusion After Failed Execution of Christa Pike

According to reporting by BBC News, newly released US emergency call records have revealed significant confusion following the failed execution attempt of death row inmate Christa Pike.

Pike, who was convicted for the murder of Colleen Slemmer, remained alive after receiving two doses of lethal injection during the scheduled procedure.

Following the failed execution attempt, officials were forced to call emergency services, and Pike was ultimately transported to a nearby hospital for medical treatment.

Readers can find the full original report on this story at BBC News.
