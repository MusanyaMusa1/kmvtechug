📷 Thieves Steal 30,000 Wine Bottles Worth €5m in Italy

According to reporting by BBC News, thieves in Italy have stolen approximately 30,000 bottles of high-value wine from a winery in Florence.

The stolen stock is estimated to be worth around €5 million, making the heist one of the largest thefts of its kind ever recorded in the country.

Authorities are looking into the circumstances surrounding the break-in as investigators work to determine how the high-value goods were removed from the Florence facility.

The full original report can be accessed online at BBC News.
