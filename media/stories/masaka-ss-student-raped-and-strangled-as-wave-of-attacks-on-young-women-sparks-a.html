Masaka SS Student Raped and Strangled as Wave of Attacks on Young Women Sparks Alarm

KM
King Musah Media News Desk
Published 13 August 2026 · English
📷 Rayati Nassali Nakato, the Masaka SS student found dead

A Senior Four candidate at Masaka Secondary School has been raped and strangled to death in a case that has intensified public alarm over the safety of young women in Uganda. The victim, identified as Rayati Nassali Nakato, was reported missing before her body was discovered. Police and local reports indicate she was sexually assaulted and strangled with a rope. The killing comes amid a series of similar incidents reported within a short period. In the space of about one week, a young woman was raped, killed and dumped in an unfinished building in Nabingo along Bataka Lane. Another case involved a housemaid who was reportedly raped and murdered by thieves in Kyampisi-Kakoola, Mukono District. Journalist Gabriel Buule, who first highlighted the cluster of cases, wrote that the pattern raises urgent questions about the safety of girls and young women across the country. These incidents have triggered widespread public concern. Many Ugandans are asking why such brutal attacks continue and whether current policing and community protection measures are adequate. The cases have also revived conversations about personal safety, the risks young women face in both urban and peri-urban settings, and the need for faster, more visible investigations. Police have not yet issued a comprehensive public statement linking the cases or detailing arrests in all of them. Investigations into Nakato’s death and the other reported incidents remain ongoing. The deaths of young women in such violent circumstances demand more than expressions of shock. They require thorough, transparent investigations, swift arrests where evidence exists, and concrete steps to improve safety for girls and women in homes, schools, workplaces and public spaces. Families of the victims deserve justice. The wider public deserves assurance that the state takes the protection of its young citizens seriously. King Musah Media will continue following the police investigations and any official updates on these cases.
