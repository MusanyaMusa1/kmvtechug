First Daughter Thanks God as First Lady Reappears After Months Away from Public Eye

MM
Musanya Musa
Published 10 August 2026 · English
📷 Natasha Museveni Karugire with First Lady Janet Museveni

First daughter Natasha Museveni Karugire has shared touching new photos with her mother, First Lady Janet Museveni, marking a welcome return to the public eye. The images, posted on Monday, show the mother and daughter together in warm, relaxed moments. Natasha captioned the post simply: “Thank you, LORD Jesus!” The First Lady had been largely absent from public functions for several months following a serious illness. President Yoweri Museveni had earlier revealed that she fell critically ill in March but was recovering well, crediting doctors and divine intervention. The fresh photos have been widely shared across social media, with many Ugandans expressing joy at seeing the First Lady looking healthy and smiling alongside her daughter. The family moment comes as a relief to many who had followed the First Lady’s health updates with concern.
