Kyaggwe Crowned 2026 Masaza Cup Champions as Kabaka Hands Over Trophy

KM
King Musah Media News Desk
Published 01 November 2026 · English
📷 Kabaka Ronald Muwenda Mutebi II handing over the Masaza Cup trophy to the Kyaggwe captain at Muteesa II Stadium

Kyaggwe County have been crowned champions of the 2026 Buganda Masaza Cup after defeating Gomba in a tightly contested final played on Saturday at Muteesa II Stadium, Wankulukuku.

The decisive moment came in the 78th minute when Kato scored the only goal of the match, sealing Kyaggwe’s first-ever Masaza Cup title since the tournament began involving all 18 traditional counties of Buganda in 2004.

His Majesty the Kabaka of Buganda, Ronald Muwenda Mutebi II, attended the final as guest of honour and personally handed over the trophy to the Kyaggwe team captain in a colourful ceremony marked by traditional drums, cultural performances and a packed stadium.

Gomba, who were appearing in their 11th final and chasing a sixth title, fell short despite a strong campaign. Third-placed Butambala completed the podium.

Prize money was awarded as follows: Kyaggwe received UGX 25 million, runners-up Gomba took home UGX 22 million, while Butambala received UGX 20 million. All three teams were also presented with medals.

Speaking after the match, officials thanked the Kabaka for gracing the occasion and reminded the public that beyond the competition, the Masaza Cup continues to create awareness in the fight against HIV/AIDS, with a special call for men to take the lead in protecting the girl child.

The atmosphere at Wankulukuku was electric as thousands of Baganda from different counties turned up to support their teams and witness the presence of their King.
