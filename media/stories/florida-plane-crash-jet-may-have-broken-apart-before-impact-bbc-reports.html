📷 Florida Plane Crash: Jet May Have Broken Apart Before Impact, BBC Reports

According to reporting by BBC News, officials investigating a fatal plane crash in Florida say the jet may have broken apart in mid-air before it nosedived to the ground.

A family of four has been identified among the victims of the tragedy. The deceased include Adam Robinson, Helen Sharp, and their two young children, four-year-old Emma and two-year-old Charlie.

Officials continue to examine the circumstances surrounding the sudden descent and the structural condition of the aircraft leading up to the impact.

Readers can find the full original report on BBC News.

Source: BBC News
