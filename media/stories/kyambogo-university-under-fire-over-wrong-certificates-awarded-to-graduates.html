Kyambogo University Under Fire Over Wrong Certificates Awarded to Graduates

KM
King Musah Media News Desk
Published 31 July 2026 · English
📷 kyambongo university leadership befre the committe

Kampala, Uganda — Kyambogo University has come under scrutiny after Members of Parliament discovered that some graduates were awarded certificates for courses they never studied. The revelation emerged during a session of the Parliamentary Committee on Education and Sports. Lawmakers heard that students admitted for programmes leading to Diplomas in Physical Education and Sports instead received certificates in Sports Management.

Vice Chancellor Prof Justus Kwetegyeka appeared before the committee and acknowledged the anomaly. He told MPs that the university is already taking steps to correct the error through its standard internal processes.

Details of the Anomaly

According to information presented to the committee, the mix-up occurred in the awarding of diplomas. Students who enrolled and completed studies under the Physical Education and Sports curriculum were issued credentials for Sports Management — a related but distinct programme.Such errors raise serious questions about quality assurance, academic records management, and the integrity of university certificates. Affected graduates may face challenges when seeking employment, further studies, or professional registration, as their official documents do not accurately reflect the courses they completed.

University Response

Prof Kwetegyeka assured the committee that the institution is addressing the matter. The university has initiated corrective procedures, which typically involve verification of academic records, possible re-issuance of accurate certificates, and internal reviews to prevent similar occurrences in the future.The Vice Chancellor emphasised that the process is being handled according to established university regulations and academic standards.

Broader Concerns

This incident adds to ongoing discussions about administrative efficiency and academic standards in public universities. MPs on the Education and Sports Committee are expected to demand clearer timelines for the correction of certificates and stronger systems to avoid future errors.For the affected students and their families, the issue has caused frustration and uncertainty. Accurate certification is critical for career progression, especially in specialised fields such as sports education and management.King Musah Media will continue following developments as Kyambogo University works to resolve the certificate discrepancies and as Parliament monitors the corrective measures.
