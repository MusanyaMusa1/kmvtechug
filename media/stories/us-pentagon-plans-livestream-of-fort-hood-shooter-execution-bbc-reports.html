📷 US Pentagon Plans Livestream of Fort Hood Shooter Execution, BBC Reports

According to a report by BBC News, the US Pentagon has announced that the upcoming execution of Fort Hood shooter Nidal Hasan will be livestreamed.

Public executions in the United States were halted nearly a century ago, making the decision to broadcast Hasan's capital punishment a notable shift in military judicial proceedings.

Legal and political experts highlighted in the report suggest that government officials may be misjudging the public mood regarding the return of publicly accessible executions.

The full report and detailed analysis can be found on BBC News.
