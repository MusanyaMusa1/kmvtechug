📷 South Africa Experiences Fresh Outbreak of Anti-Migrant Violence

Fresh anti-migrant violence has erupted in parts of South Africa, according to reporting by BBC News. The latest unrest has resulted in vehicles being torched and local shops being looted.

South Africa has experienced recurrent outbreaks of such violence in recent years. These destructive incidents frequently target migrants who have moved to the country from neighbouring African nations.

Tensions surrounding foreign nationals remain a persistent issue in the region, leading to repeated disruptions and damage to local businesses and property.

Readers can find the full original report detailing the situation on the BBC News website.
