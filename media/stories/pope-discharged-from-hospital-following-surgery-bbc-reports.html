📷 Pope Discharged From Hospital Following Surgery, BBC Reports

According to reporting by BBC News, Pope Francis has been discharged from the hospital just days after undergoing surgery for lung cancer.

The pontiff's release marks an encouraging step in his recovery following the surgical procedure.

Following his discharge, the Pope is scheduled to give the traditional Angelus prayer and blessing in St Peter's Square on Sunday.

Readers can find the full original report on the BBC News website.

Source: BBC News
