What you will study

Nine modules from foundations through policy, economics, and careers - with Uganda-specific examples, practice quizzes, and graded module quizzes.

  • 1Foundations of Environmental & Conservation Psychology
  • 2Perceiving & Valuing the Environment
  • 3Pro-Environmental Behaviour
  • 4Environmental Communication & Media
  • 5Climate Change Psychology
  • 6Community, Disasters & Conservation Behaviour
  • 7Environmental Policy & Behaviour Change
  • 8Economic Policy & Global Environmental Concerns
  • 9Applying Environmental Psychology - Careers & Practice
Grading & certificate
  • Each module has a graded quiz (70% pass, max 3 attempts).
  • Practice quizzes are unlimited and do not count toward the certificate.
  • Certificate is issued automatically when your average across all 9 module graded quizzes is 80% or higher.
  • Certificate is delivered to your email and KMVTECH Education dashboard.

Who this is for

University students in Uganda (and East Africa) in psychology, law, health sciences, social sciences, business, environmental studies, and related fields. No advanced psychology degree required - content is practical and accessible.
