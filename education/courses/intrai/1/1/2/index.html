Strong, Weak, Narrow and General AI

Text~5 min

Narrow AI (also called weak AI) is designed for a specific task. Face unlock, spam filters, or a crop-disease image classifier are narrow systems. Almost all AI you meet today is narrow.

General AI would handle a wide range of tasks at human-like flexibility. That does not exist yet in a practical form. News headlines sometimes blur this difference.

When someone says "the AI will take over," ask: which task, with which data, under whose control? Clear questions beat vague fear.
