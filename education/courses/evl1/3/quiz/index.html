Section A - Multiple Choice (8 questions)
Question 1 of 15
Which of the following best defines judicial notice?
A
A court order requiring a party to produce documents
B
A court's acceptance of certain facts as established without requiring formal proof
C
A judge's written decision at the end of a case
D
A formal notice served on a witness to appear in court
Judicial notice is the process by which a court accepts certain facts as established without requiring formal proof from the parties. It is an exception to the general rule that all facts must be proved through evidence.
Not correct. Judicial notice is the court's acceptance of certain facts without requiring formal proof. The correct answer is B.

Question 5 of 15

B
Whether both parties agree that the fact is true

The test for discretionary judicial notice is whether the fact is so notorious or so clearly established and beyond reasonable dispute that evidence would be superfluous. A judge's personal knowledge, party agreement, or media reports are not the test.

Question 7 of 15

B

The case illustrates that courts work from what is properly established before them - whether through evidence or through judicial notice of appropriate facts. Courts do not simply assume facts that are genuinely in dispute and do not fall within a recognised category of judicially noticed facts.
Not correct. The case illustrates that courts work from what is properly established and do not assume genuinely disputed facts. The correct answer is B.
Question 8 of 15

Section B - True or False (4 questions)
Question 9 of 15
Mandatory judicial notice gives the opposing party the right to produce evidence contradicting the noticed fact.
False. Mandatory judicial notice results in the conclusive acceptance of the noticed fact. The opposing party cannot produce evidence to contradict it. This conclusive effect distinguishes mandatory from discretionary judicial notice.
False is correct. Mandatorily noticed facts are conclusively established - they cannot be contradicted by evidence.
Question 10 of 15
A Ugandan court is required to take judicial notice of the provisions of the Constitution of Uganda 1995.
True. The Constitution of Uganda 1995 is a law in force in Uganda. Courts take mandatory judicial notice of all laws in force in Uganda, which includes the Constitution.
True is correct. Courts take mandatory judicial notice of all laws in force in Uganda, including the Constitution.
Question 11 of 15
A lawyer who cites the Evidence Act as Cap. 6 instead of Cap. 8 is correctly invoking judicial notice of the statute.
False. Courts take judicial notice of what the law actually is, including the correct chapter numbers under the 2024 Revised Edition. Citing Cap. 6 is citing the wrong or non-existent statute. Incorrect citations are not saved by judicial notice.
False is correct. Judicial notice covers what the law actually is. Citing the wrong Cap number cites the wrong statute.
Question 12 of 15
Discretionary judicial notice can, in principle, be challenged by a party who disputes the noticed fact.
True. Unlike mandatory judicial notice which is conclusive, facts accepted under discretionary judicial notice can in principle be challenged. The best time to challenge is before the court takes notice, not after proceedings have moved on.
True is correct. Discretionarily noticed facts can in principle be challenged, unlike mandatory judicial notice which is conclusive.

Section D - Fill in the Blank (2 questions)
Question 14 of 15 - Fill in the Blank
Judicial notice is divided into two categories. Facts a court is legally required to accept without proof fall under _______ judicial notice. Facts a court may accept without proof but is not required to fall under _______ judicial notice. (Type either 'mandatory' or 'discretionary' to complete either blank.)
Type either 'mandatory' or 'discretionary'.
Mandatory judicial notice requires the court to accept the fact - it has no discretion to demand proof. Discretionary judicial notice allows the court to accept the fact without proof but the court may instead require formal proof if it considers that appropriate.
The two answers are 'mandatory' and 'discretionary'.
Question 15 of 15 - Fill in the Blank
When a lawyer cites an Act of Parliament in a Ugandan court, they do not need to prove it exists because courts take _______ judicial notice of all laws in force in Uganda. However, the lawyer must use the correct _______ number under the 2024 Revised Edition to identify the statute accurately. (Type either answer.)
Type either 'mandatory' or 'cap' (as in Cap number).
Courts take mandatory judicial notice of all laws in force in Uganda. The lawyer must use the correct Cap number from the 2024 Revised Edition (for example Evidence Act Cap. 8, Penal Code Act Cap. 128) because judicial notice applies to what the law actually is, not to an incorrect citation.
The answers are 'mandatory' and 'cap'.
