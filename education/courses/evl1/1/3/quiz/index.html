Question 1 of 5
Uganda's evidence law traces its origins primarily to:
A
Roman-Dutch law
B
French civil law
C
English common law codified in the Indian Evidence Act 1872
D
American constitutional law
Correct. Uganda's evidence law traces its origins to English common law codified in the Indian Evidence Act of 1872.
Not correct. Uganda's evidence law comes from English common law via the Indian Evidence Act 1872. The correct answer is C.
