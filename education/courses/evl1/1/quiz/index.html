Question 24 of 25
What is the relationship between the Evidence Act Cap. 8 and the Civil Procedure Act Cap. 282?
A
They are the same statute
B
The Civil Procedure Act replaces the Evidence Act in civil cases
C
The Civil Procedure Act governs civil procedure generally while the Evidence Act governs how facts are proved within those proceedings
D
The Evidence Act only applies to proceedings under the Civil Procedure Act
Correct. The Civil Procedure Act governs civil proceedings generally. The Evidence Act governs how facts are proved within those proceedings. They work together but serve different functions.
Not correct. These are separate statutes. The Civil Procedure Act governs procedure generally; the Evidence Act governs proof of facts. The correct answer is C.
