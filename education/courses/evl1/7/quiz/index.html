Section A - Multiple Choice (10 questions)
Question 1 of 20
What is a legal presumption?
A
A judge's personal view about disputed facts
B
A rule allowing a court to take a fact as established once a primary fact is proved without direct proof of the presumed fact
C
An agreement between parties about undisputed facts
D
An expert witness opinion about what probably occurred
A presumption is a rule of law allowing or requiring a court to take a fact as established once a primary fact is proved - without requiring direct proof of the presumed fact.
Not correct. A presumption is a rule of law allowing a court to take a fact as established once a primary fact is proved. The correct answer is B.
Question 2 of 20
What primary fact triggers the presumption of death under Section 107 of the Evidence Act Cap. 8?
A
Ten years of absence from Uganda
B
A police missing persons report more than five years old
C
The person has not been heard of for seven years by those who would naturally have heard of them if alive
D
A medical report confirming probable death
Section 107 requires that the person has not been heard of for seven years by those who would naturally have heard of them if alive.
Not correct. Section 107 requires seven years of absence from those who would naturally have heard. The correct answer is C.
Question 3 of 20

B
Presumption of legitimacy - he must adduce evidence of non-access during the period of conception
C

The presumption of legitimacy operates. To rebut it the husband must adduce evidence of non-access - that he had no sexual access to the wife during the period when the child was conceived.
Not correct. The presumption of legitimacy operates and is rebutted by evidence of non-access. The correct answer is B.

Question 5 of 20

Question 6 of 20

The irrebuttable presumption under the Penal Code Act Cap. 128 treats children under twelve as conclusively incapable of committing a crime. At eleven the child falls within this conclusive presumption and proceedings must terminate.
Not correct. The irrebuttable presumption under the Penal Code Act Cap. 128 conclusively treats children under twelve as incapable of crime. The correct answer is B.
Question 7 of 20

A
A presumption that applies only in civil cases
B
A presumption that operates unless and until displaced by sufficient contrary evidence
C
A presumption that cannot be challenged under any circumstances

A rebuttable presumption operates unless and until displaced by sufficient contrary evidence. Once sufficient contrary evidence is adduced the presumption ceases and the court decides on all evidence.
Not correct. A rebuttable presumption operates unless displaced by sufficient contrary evidence. The correct answer is B.

Question 10 of 20

Section B - True or False (5 questions)
Question 11 of 20
An irrebuttable presumption can be displaced by particularly cogent evidence on the balance of probabilities.
False. An irrebuttable presumption is conclusive - it cannot be displaced by any evidence however cogent. Once the primary fact is established the presumed fact is taken as absolutely true as a matter of law.
False is correct. Irrebuttable presumptions cannot be displaced by any evidence.
Question 12 of 20
Section 107 of the Evidence Act Cap. 8 creates a rebuttable presumption of death after seven years of absence.
True. Section 107 creates the presumption of death where a person has not been heard of for seven years by those who would naturally have heard of them if alive. It is rebuttable by evidence that the person is alive.
True is correct. Section 107 creates a rebuttable presumption of death after seven years.
Question 13 of 20
The presumption of legitimacy means a child born outside marriage is presumed illegitimate until the father acknowledges paternity.
False. The presumption of legitimacy specifically applies to children born during a lawful marriage - it presumes the husband is the father. It does not apply to children born outside marriage.
False is correct. The presumption of legitimacy applies to children born during a lawful marriage, not outside marriage.
Question 14 of 20
The presumption of innocence under Article 28(3)(a) of the Constitution of Uganda 1995 can be overridden by specific statutory provisions.
False. The presumption of innocence is a constitutional guarantee. No inferior legislation or statutory provision can override a constitutional guarantee. Statutes must be read consistently with constitutional rights.
False is correct. The constitutional presumption of innocence cannot be overridden by statute.
Question 15 of 20
The presumption of regularity means all official acts are presumed properly done but this can be rebutted by evidence of irregularity.
True. The presumption of regularity - omnia praesumuntur rite esse acta - presumes all official and judicial acts were properly done. It is rebuttable by evidence of irregularity or excess of authority.
True is correct. The presumption of regularity is rebuttable by evidence of irregularity.

Section D - Fill in the Blank (3 questions)
Question 18 of 20 - Fill in the Blank
A _______ presumption can be displaced by contrary evidence. A _______ presumption cannot be challenged regardless of the evidence produced.
Type either 'rebuttable' or 'irrebuttable'.
Rebuttable presumptions can be displaced by contrary evidence. Irrebuttable (conclusive) presumptions cannot be challenged once the primary fact is established.
The answers are 'rebuttable' and 'irrebuttable'.
Question 19 of 20 - Fill in the Blank
Section _______ of the Evidence Act Cap. 8 creates the presumption of death when a person has not been heard of for _______ years.
Type either the section number or the number of years.
Section 107 of the Evidence Act Cap. 8 creates the presumption of death. The threshold is seven years of absence without news from those who would naturally have heard.
The answers are 107 and seven.
Question 20 of 20 - Fill in the Blank
The presumption of innocence is guaranteed by Article _______ of the Constitution of Uganda 1995. It is displaced only by proof _______ _______ _______.
Type the article number or the standard required to displace the presumption.
Article 28(3)(a) of the Constitution of Uganda 1995 guarantees the presumption of innocence. It is rebuttable only by proof beyond reasonable doubt.
The answers are 28(3)(a) and beyond reasonable doubt.
