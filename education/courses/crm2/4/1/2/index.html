Assault occasioning actual bodily harm

ReadingCRM2

Where assault results in actual bodily harm - bruises, swelling, or recognised psychiatric injury - liability rises (section 219 framework). R v Roberts (persuasive) shows that injuries suffered while escaping an assault can still be attributed to the assailant.

Market fight example: a vendor is slapped and shoved, left bruised and distressed - ABH territory rather than mere common assault.

Punishment maxima are higher than for common assault; always cite the current Cap. 128 section and maximum when advising.
