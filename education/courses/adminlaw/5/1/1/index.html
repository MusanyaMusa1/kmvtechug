Following the modern restatement (illegality, irrationality/unreasonableness, procedural impropriety) helps structure answers. Illegality includes ultra vires, wrong purpose, irrelevant considerations, fettering discretion and error of law. Irrationality is high threshold. Procedural impropriety covers natural justice and statutory procedure.
