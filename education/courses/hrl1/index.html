Uganda 2024 Revised Edition

Human Rights Law

Law Uganda 2024 Acts Cited

Master human rights in Uganda: Constitution Chapter Four, African and UN systems, limitation, enforcement and vulnerable groups.

The Big Transformation

After this course you will be able to locate rights in the Constitution and treaties, apply the limitation test, choose the right enforcement forum and design practical remedies for rights violations in Uganda.

Who This Course Is For

LLB students (especially Year 3-4), bar candidates, NGO and public-interest practitioners, and public officers who work with rights issues.

Prerequisites and Recommended Sequence

No prior knowledge required beyond secondary school education. Constitutional Law is helpful but not mandatory.

What You Will Learn - Module Breakdown

  • Module 1: Concepts of human rights - Universality, generations of rights, duties and the African Charter approach.
  • Module 2: UN system - Charter and treaty bodies, UPR, individual communications and soft law.
  • Module 3: African system - African Charter, Commission, Court and specialised protocols.
  • Module 4: Uganda Bill of Rights - Chapter Four of the 1995 Constitution, key rights and Article 20.
  • Module 5: Limitation and derogation - Article 43 test, proportionality, non-derogable rights and emergencies.
  • Module 6: Enforcement - Article 50, UHRC, structural interdicts and regional routes.
  • Module 7: Selected rights - Life, liberty, equality and freedom of expression in practice.
  • Module 8: Vulnerable groups and accountability - Women, children, disability, detention and closing protection gaps.

Assessment and Certificate

Each module ends with a graded quiz. You need 70% to pass each module and an 80% average across all modules to earn your certificate. You may retake quizzes. Progress is saved when you are logged in and enrolled.

Time Commitment

Approximately 30-40 hours. Most students finish in 4-7 weeks part-time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes. The course is built around current Ugandan law and typical exam-style application. Use it alongside your faculty materials and past papers.

Do I need to complete another course before this one?

See the Prerequisites section above. Most law courses can be taken independently, though the recommended sequence helps.

Yes. The site is mobile-responsive. A floating Next Lesson button appears on small screens.

Will I get a certificate even if I fail some modules and retake?

Yes. You may retake graded quizzes. Once you reach the required scores, the certificate is issued automatically.
