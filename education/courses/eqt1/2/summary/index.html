Module 2 Summary – Equitable Remedies

Summary

In this module you have studied the principal coercive and restorative remedies of Equity and the main defences that can defeat them.

Remedies covered

  • Specific Performance – discretionary; adequacy of damages test; bars include personal service and constant supervision.
  • Injunctions – prohibitory and mandatory; temporary injunctions governed by Kiyimba Kaggwa v Katende.
  • Rescission – restores parties to pre-contract position.
  • Rectification – corrects the written instrument to match true agreement.
  • Account, Delivery Up and Declarations.

Defences covered

  • Laches and Acquiescence
  • Clean Hands
  • Hardship, lack of mutuality, illegality, third-party rights

Key cases

  • Kiyimba Kaggwa v Katende (temporary injunctions)
  • American Cyanamid Co v Ethicon Ltd (persuasive)
  • Bank of Uganda v Hassan Bassajabalaba (clean hands)
