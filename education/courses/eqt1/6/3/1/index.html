How the Modules Fit Together – A Practical Roadmap

Video Summary

The six modules move from foundations and remedies, through the creation and classification of trusts, to the duties of trustees and finally to tracing and remedies for breach. Together they provide a complete analytical framework.

Module 1 gave you the foundations: the history of Equity, the maxims, and the concurrent administration of law and equity under the Judicature Act Cap 16 and the Constitution.

Module 2 covered the main equitable remedies: specific performance, injunctions, rescission, rectification and others.

Module 3 examined the nature and creation of trusts: the three certainties, formalities, and constitution.

Module 4 classified trusts: express, resulting, constructive and charitable.

Module 5 dealt with the office of trustee: appointment, duties, powers and breach.

Module 6 has shown you how to follow and trace misapplied property and how to choose personal and proprietary remedies, including claims against third parties.

Together these modules equip you to analyse a typical Equity and Trusts problem.

Key Points

  • Modules 1–2: foundations and equitable remedies.
  • Modules 3–4: creation and classification of trusts.
  • Modules 5–6: trusteeship, breach, tracing and remedies.
