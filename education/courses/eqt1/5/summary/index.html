Module 5 Summary – Trusteeship and Duties of Trustees

Summary

In this module you have studied the office of trustee and the core obligations that attach to it.

Appointment, Retirement and Removal

  • Trustees are appointed by the settlor, the court, or under the Trustees Act Cap 270.
  • Retirement and removal must leave a sufficient number of trustees and focus on the welfare of beneficiaries.

Core Duties

  • Duty of care – prudent person standard; higher for professionals.
  • Fiduciary loyalty – no conflict, no unauthorised profit.
  • Duty to invest, keep accounts and provide information.

Powers and Breach

  • Administrative and dispositive powers must be exercised properly.
  • Breach leads to personal liability; defences and statutory relief may be available.
  • Proprietary remedies are developed further in Module 6.
