Module 1 Summary – Foundations of Equity in Uganda

Text

This module has laid the essential foundations for the entire course.

What you should now understand

  • Equity developed in England to soften the harshness of the common law.
  • The Earl of Oxford's Case (1615) established that Equity prevails in case of conflict.
  • Equity was received into Uganda through the 1902 and 1911 Orders in Council.
  • Today it is preserved by section 14 of the Judicature Act Cap 16 and reinforced by Article 126(2)(e) of the Constitution.
  • The twelve Maxims of Equity are the guiding principles used by courts.
  • Courts administer common law and Equity concurrently; Equity prevails in conflict.

Key statutory provisions

  • Judicature Act Cap 16, section 14(2)(b) and 14(4)
  • Constitution of Uganda, Article 126(2)(e)

Key cases

  • Earl of Oxford's Case (1615)
  • Stephen Mabosi v Uganda Revenue Authority
  • Bank of Uganda v Hassan Bassajabalaba
  • Katarikawe v William Katwiremu
