Private Express Trusts – Fixed and Discretionary

Express Trusts

An express trust is one that is deliberately created by the settlor, either by transferring property to a trustee or by declaring himself trustee. Express trusts may be private or charitable.

Fixed Trusts

In a fixed trust the beneficiaries and their shares are predetermined by the settlor. The trustee has no discretion as to who takes or in what proportions. The complete list test applies for certainty of objects.

Discretionary Trusts

In a discretionary trust the trustees have power to select who among a defined class will benefit and in what shares. The any-given-postulant test from McPhail v Doulton applies. Discretionary trusts are flexible and are often used for tax planning, asset protection and family provision.

Key Points

  • Express trusts are deliberately created by the settlor.
  • Fixed trusts – beneficiaries and shares predetermined; complete list test.
  • Discretionary trusts – trustees select beneficiaries; any given postulant test.
