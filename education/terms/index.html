1. About these terms

KMVTECH Education is an online learning platform for university students, run by KMVTECHUG in Kampala, Uganda. We call ourselves "we" and "us". We call you "you" or "the student".

When you register, log in or enroll, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the platform.

You must be 18 or older, or have the permission of a parent or guardian, to register.

2. Your account

  • Registering is free and gives you an account and a student number. It does not give you access to course lessons. For that you need a paid plan (section 3).
  • Give us true and complete details, and keep your password private. You are responsible for what happens on your account.
  • One account is for one person. Do not share your login with anyone.
  • Your full name, email address and student number can only be changed by an administrator. This protects the accuracy of your certificate. If a detail is wrong, contact us (section 14).
  • You must verify your email address before you can log in. We use it to send you important messages about your account.
  • We may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms (section 7).

4. Access and your progress

  • A paid plan lets you open the lessons, videos, notes, quizzes and certificates it covers, during the period you paid for.
  • Courses you complete. If you earn the certificate for a course during a paid period, you keep access to that course and its certificate for as long as the course remains on the platform. Your certificate stays in your account.
  • When a plan ends, you lose access to courses you have not completed. Your progress is saved so you can continue if you pay again. We save it for 12 months after your last activity, or 6 months if you had barely started. After that we archive it. We do not delete it, and you can restore it by buying access again.
  • Access is for you only, on your own devices.

5. Quizzes and certificates

  • Practice quizzes can be repeated as often as you like. They are for learning and do not count towards your certificate.
  • Graded module quizzes allow up to 3 attempts. After 3 unsuccessful attempts you must wait 24 hours before trying again. Your best score for each module counts.
  • You pass a module with 70%.
  • You earn a course certificate when your average across all the module quizzes in that course is 80% or more, during an active paid period. The certificate is created automatically.
  • Each certificate shows your registered full name (in capital letters), your student number, the course, the date, your score and a unique verification code.
  • If you have a certificate and want a higher score on it, you may retake the quizzes. A new certificate with the improved score costs between UGX 3,000 and UGX 5,000.
  • We may cancel a certificate that was issued by mistake or obtained by cheating (section 7).
  • Verification. Anyone who has your certificate code can use our public verification page to check it. That page shows your name, student number, course, date issued and score.
Study hours shown on your transcript are a record of your learning. They are not official professional development (CPD) points unless we say so in writing.

6. Refunds

Access to digital courses starts as soon as we activate it, so payments are generally not refundable. We will refund or fix the problem in these cases:

  • You paid, and we did not activate your access within 48 hours of confirming your payment.
  • You were charged twice for the same plan. We refund the extra payment.
  • A course you paid for for a single course is withdrawn before you could use it.

To ask for a refund, contact us on WhatsApp or email within 7 days of the payment, with your student number and payment details.

10. Privacy and your data

What we collect

  • Account details: your full name, email address, phone number and student number.
  • Learning records: the pages you open, your progress, quiz attempts and scores, and your certificates.

Why we use it

To create and protect your account, verify your email, activate your plan, track your progress, issue and verify certificates, answer your questions, keep the platform safe, and improve our courses. We do not sell your personal data.

We use trusted services to run the platform: Supabase (accounts and database), Resend (emails), GitHub Pages (website hosting), YouTube (video) and Google tools such as Google Analytics and Search Console. They handle data for us under their own terms and security practices. We share only what is needed for the service.

We keep your account and learning records while your account is active and for as long as needed for the purposes above. Progress is kept as described in section 4. Payment records are kept as long as the law or our accounts require. Certificates and their verification records are kept so that they can still be checked later.

Contact us in section 14. We may need to confirm who you are first.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data. No online service is completely secure. Please use a strong password, and tell us quickly if you think someone else has used your account.
