WHY WE EXIST

Digital access is not the same as digital ability.

Having a smartphone, computer or internet connection does not automatically mean someone can use digital tools independently.

Everyday tasks such as creating a document, using email, sharing files, preparing a presentation or recognising an online scam can still be difficult. Digital Independence responds to this gap through practical, hands-on learning built around real-life needs.

OUR JOURNEY

One community learning experience became a bigger commitment.

Digital Independence grew out of Inclusive Digital Skills, an individual project developed through the Millennium Fellowship Class of 2026. A community engagement on 17 October 2026 helped reveal the value of practical, accessible digital learning.

INCLUSION IS PART OF THE DESIGN

Digital skills should be within reach.

Our intended learners include secondary students, university students, young people and adults. The initiative is open to different starting points, with deliberate attention to accessibility and the participation of persons with physical disabilities.
