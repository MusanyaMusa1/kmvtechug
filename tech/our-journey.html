OUR JOURNEY

A community learning experience. A commitment to keep building.

Digital Independence grew out of Inclusive Digital Skills, an individual project developed through the Millennium Fellowship Class of 2026.

WHERE IT BEGAN

Inclusive Digital Skills

The starting point was an individual project focused on practical digital skills and inclusion. It was developed through the Millennium Fellowship Class of 2026, with Quality Education (Sustainable Development Goal 4) as its primary SDG focus.

The Fellowship experience provided a framework for thinking about community engagement, learning and the role of practical digital skills in widening participation.

The project’s participation in the Millennium Fellowship is a description of its development context. It should not be understood as an endorsement, sponsorship or operation by the Millennium Campus Network or the United Nations Academic Impact.

2026

PROJECT DEVELOPMENT

Inclusive Digital Skills takes shape.

An individual community-focused project was developed through the Millennium Fellowship Class of 2026, with attention to practical digital skills, online safety and inclusion.

17 OCT 2026

COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT

Learning through a community session.

A community engagement was held in the founder’s village. The session introduced participants to the Sustainable Development Goals and the Millennium Fellowship, and explored practical topics including responsible online sharing, personal information, two-factor authentication for WhatsApp and email, online scams, educational tutorials and using online platforms to market small businesses.

Participants also took part in group activities. The attendance register recorded 38 participants.

AFTER THE SESSION

WHAT THE EXPERIENCE REVEALED

One event could not meet every need.

The engagement showed the value of practical digital learning and the importance of creating opportunities for people to practise. It also highlighted delivery challenges, including limited venue space, power interruptions, different levels of digital experience and the time required to support participants individually.

The experience strengthened the case for a more sustained initiative rather than treating the session as a one-off endpoint.

NEXT CHAPTER

DIGITAL INDEPENDENCE

A broader initiative begins to develop.

Digital Independence Initiative was developed from that experience, with a clearer identity, a four-pillar learning framework and a volunteer team. Its website and team structure are part of building the foundation for future work.

Further community training activities are still to be developed and resourced. We do not present them as already delivered.

OUR COMMITMENT

Build carefully. Be honest about progress.

We want to grow in a way that is practical, inclusive and accountable. That means being clear about what has happened, what is being developed and what still requires resources and partnerships.
