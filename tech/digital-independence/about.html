ABOUT THE INITIATIVE

Digital confidence is built through practice.

We want people to move beyond simply having access to technology and become more capable of using it for everyday life.

OUR PURPOSE

Making everyday digital tasks less intimidating.

Digital tools shape how people learn, communicate, work and access opportunities. Yet many people still depend on others to complete basic digital tasks.

Digital Independence Initiative responds to this gap by focusing on practical skills, confidence and safer digital behaviour. Learning is intended to be active: participants practise tasks, ask questions, make mistakes and try again.

The initiative is a community impact initiative under KMVTECH. It has its own focus on digital inclusion and practical learning.

OUR PRINCIPLES

How we want learning to feel.

01

Practical

Skills are connected to tasks people may need to complete in real life, not only explanations or demonstrations.

02

Inclusive

People start from different levels of experience. Learning should recognise those differences and consider accessibility.

03

Independent

The goal is not just to complete a task once, but to build the confidence to repeat it and solve simple problems.

WHO WE AIM TO SERVE

Different learners. Everyday digital needs.

Secondary students
Foundational skills for learning and independent digital life.

University students
Digital competencies for study, collaboration and opportunity.

Young people and adults
Practical skills for communication, work, learning and safer participation.

Persons with disabilities
Accessibility and meaningful participation are considerations in how activities are planned.
