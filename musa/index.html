Publications

Legal & Policy Writing

Published in the Uganda Law Students Association (ULSA) Journal, a print publication.

ULSA Journal · Published 2025

The Role of Digital Platforms in Promoting Citizens' Advocacy in Uganda

Examines how platforms like X, TikTok, WhatsApp, and Facebook have reshaped civic participation among Uganda's youth, and the legal tension between digital freedoms, state surveillance, and constitutional protections under Articles 29, 38, and 41.

ULSA Journal · Accepted for 2026 edition

AI Governance in Uganda: Legal Gaps and the Case for a Contextual Regulatory Framework

Explores the legal risks of AI in Uganda's 2025–2026 electoral period — including deepfakes, biometric surveillance, and algorithmic exclusion — and proposes a rights-based regulatory framework.

ULSA Journal · Accepted for 2026 edition

Bridging the Gap Between Law and Practice: Electoral Administration in Uganda's 2026 Elections

Analyzes the disconnect between electoral law and its implementation — from biometric kit failures to internet shutdowns — and proposes reforms drawing on Kenyan, Indian, and Estonian models.

ULSA Journal · Under review

Beyond Physical Harm: Occupational Safety, Mental Health, and Informal Labour in Uganda

Evaluates Uganda's Occupational Safety and Health (Amendment) Act, 2025, and the gap in legal protection for the 89% of the workforce in the informal economy.
